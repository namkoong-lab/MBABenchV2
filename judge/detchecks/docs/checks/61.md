## Rule as implemented

**Fail** when the sheets of the delivered workbook do not all open at the same zoom level.
House Standards: "One zoom level throughout"; rubric: "Every sheet should use the same zoom
level across the entire workbook". Whole workbook, case tabs included, no starting-file diff.

| point | decision |
|---|---|
| which zoom | the zoom the sheet **opens at**: `<sheetView zoomScale>` of the sheet's view for workbook window 0, else its first view (`SheetHead.view`). ECMA-376 defines zoomScale as the zoom of the *current* view, whatever the view mode |
| missing zoomScale | 100 (schema default). Toy T3 rests on this: a sheet with no zoomScale opens at 100% |
| `zoomScale="0"` | 100, as the core's `SheetView.zoom` treats it |
| no `<sheetView>` | 100 |
| `zoomScaleNormal`, `zoomScalePageLayoutView`, `zoomScaleSheetLayoutView` | per-mode memories. Excel uses them only when the user switches view mode, and **never for the opening zoom**: the toy Overview (61 T1/T3) verifies that a sheet with only zoomScaleNormal=80 shows 100%. They never decide; stats record the alternative "per-view" reading (`ZOOM_READING`) |
| page layout / page break preview | judged by zoomScale like any sheet. A page-break-preview sheet that opens at 60% among 80% sheets fails; one at 80% passes. Listed in `stats.non_normal_views` |
| tolerance | none (`ZOOM_TOLERANCE = 0`): 79% next to 80% is two levels. The report's 2-point tolerance existed to forgive inherited case tabs, and those are now graded |
| sheets counted | worksheets, dialog sheets and macro sheets, i.e. sheets with a cell grid, **hidden and very hidden ones included** (`INCLUDE_HIDDEN_SHEETS = True`, whole workbook) |
| chart sheets | **not counted** (`COUNTED_KINDS`). A chart sheet has no grid; its zoom sizes a chart page (often `zoomToFit`). Each is listed in `stats.excluded_sheets` with its zoom. The LLM judge is never served chart-sheet zooms either |
| extra workbook windows | only window 0's view decides. Sheets with views for more windows are listed in `stats.sheets_with_extra_window_views` |
| `<sheetViews>` after `<sheetData>` | schema-invalid element order; Excel does not open such a file as-is (it offers a repair). The sheet's opening zoom is **unknown** (see "Unknown zoom" below), not 100. Listed in `stats.sheet_views_after_sheet_data`. Review fix 2026-10-04 |

Only sheet heads are read; sheet data is skipped at byte level (the engine still parses the tail after `</sheetData>`, which is how a late `<sheetViews>` is noticed in `sheet_end`).

### Mistakes

- The **reference level** is the level most counted sheets open at. In a tie, it is the level of the earliest such sheet in tab order.
- There is one mistake per sheet at another level. The location is the bare sheet name. The description gives the sheet's zoom, why it is that zoom ("stores no zoomScale, so Excel opens it at the default 100%", "zoomScaleNormal=80 is not the opening zoom"), and how many sheets open at the reference level. Severity is minor.
- The summary lists every level with its sheets.

Stats:
- per sheet and per level: `zooms`, `levels`, `reference_zoom`
- `sheets_without_zoomScale`, `zoomScaleNormal_differs`, `non_normal_views`, `hidden_sheets_counted`, `excluded_sheets`, `sheets_with_extra_window_views`
- `unreadable_stats_only_attributes`
- `other_reading`: the per-view reading's decision and its deviating sheets
- `reading_sensitive`: the two readings disagree
- `undecidable_sheets`: counted sheets whose opening zoom is unknown, with the reason
- `sheet_views_after_sheet_data`: sheets (counted or not) whose `<sheetViews>` follows `<sheetData>`

### Unknown zoom: raise only when it decides (no guess)

A counted sheet's zoomScale that is not an integer, or is outside 10..400 (but not 0), gives an unknown opening zoom. So does a `<sheetViews>` that follows `<sheetData>` (2026-10-04). Such a sheet:
- never gets a mistake and is left out of `zooms`/`levels`; it is listed in `stats.undecidable_sheets` and named in the summary ("the verdict does not depend on it");
- makes the check raise GradingError **only when it could change the verdict**: when the readable sheets do not already differ by more than 2 × `ZOOM_TOLERANCE` (with tolerance 0: when they all share one level, or there are none). If they already differ, no value of the unknown zoom can make the workbook one level, so the verdict is fail. If it is the only counted sheet, the verdict is pass (one sheet cannot differ from itself).

Review fix 2026-10-03 (finding `61-raises-although-already-failing`): before, any bad zoomScale raised straight away, even when two other sheets already differed.

Review fix 2026-10-04 (finding `61-sheetviews-after-sheetdata-silent`): before, a sheet whose `<sheetViews>` came after `<sheetData>` was read as having no view and silently taken as 100%. How it is noticed without touching the core: the engine parses the tail between `sheet_start` and `sheet_end` and applies a late `<sheetViews>` to the same `SheetHead`, so `sheet_end` compares `head.views` with what `sheet_start` saw; a change means the views came late. The sheet is then moved from the counted rows to `undecidable_sheets` and the rule above applies (fail if the readable sheets already differ, pass if it is the only counted sheet, else GradingError). A late `<sheetViews>` on an excluded chart sheet is stats only. The census `scratch/rereview/61/late_views_scan.py` found 0 such sheet parts in the 374 corpus files (4,655 parts), so no corpus verdict changes. Tests: `test_61_sheet_views_after_sheet_data_is_unknown`.

A sheet part that cannot be read raises in the engine. A bad per-mode attribute never raises, because it is stats only. Neither does a bad zoom on an excluded chart sheet.

## Edge-case decisions

- **Hidden sheets count.** Rubric: "every sheet ... across the entire workbook". House Standards: "throughout". The reader's ground rule is "whole workbook". A hidden sheet at another zoom therefore fails 61 as well as 92.
- **Chart sheets do not count.** There is no grid to keep at one scale, and the zoom of a `zoomToFit` chart sheet follows the window.
- **Ties** go to the earliest sheet's level. This only changes *which* sheets are named, never the decision.
- **Corpus attempts (71, census of the prototype's view dump):** no chart sheets, no hidden sheets, no non-normal views, no odd zoom values. 31 sheets have zoomScale ≠ zoomScaleNormal and 2 have zoomScaleNormal only.

## Known limits

- Excel's behaviour is assumed for two cases: zoomScale absent while a page-layout or page-break-preview per-mode zoom is set, and zoomScale="0". Neither occurs in the toys or the corpus attempts.
- Custom views (`customSheetViews`) are not read (reader limit). They are not the live view.
- The late-`<sheetViews>` detection relies on the engine applying a tail `<sheetViews>` to the same `SheetHead` (`core/sheet.py apply_top_level`, shared by head and tail parsing). If the reader ever ignores late elements instead, the detection goes quiet and `test_61_sheet_views_after_sheet_data_is_unknown` fails loudly; the right home for the detection is then the reader (raise on schema-invalid order), as the reviewer proposed.
- Which attribute Excel displays when zoomScale and zoomScaleNormal are both present and differ is **not measured** (question 4). The toys cannot tell the readings apart (`reading_sensitive` false on all six).
